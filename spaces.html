Spaces

Neue Zukunft

Berlin Friedrichshain, since 2024

On new found premises Neue Zukunft continues the work of Zukunft am Ostkreuz.
Awareded with the Tag Der Clubkultur price by Berlin Club Commission in 2024 and 2026 and the APPLAUS Award by Initiative Musik in 2025.

Zukunft am Ostkreuz

Berlin Friedrichshain, 2014-2023

Music venue, theatre and bar. Due to development plans it was closed and relocated in 2023.

Patchworx

Berlin Neukölln, 2016-2022

Community oriented project space that includes four rehearsal rooms, a photo-lab and and open workshop for different crafts.

Bei Ruth

Berlin Neukölln, 2014-2018

Non-profit and collectively organized music venue. Continues to be operated by new collectives under different names.

Club Schilli

Ulm, 2008-2011

Where it all began. Self-organized youth center under the wings of CVJM and SJR Ulm.
